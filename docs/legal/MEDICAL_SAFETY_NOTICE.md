# Medical and Safety Notice

**Last Updated: May 21, 2026**

Sturdy is a parenting support tool, not a medical or mental health service. This notice explains when you should seek professional help instead of or in addition to using Sturdy.

---

## Sturdy Is Not Medical Advice

Nothing in Sturdy — including scripts, answers to questions, and communication suggestions — constitutes medical advice, psychological treatment, therapy, or clinical diagnosis. The AI-generated content is intended as general parenting support only.

Do not rely on Sturdy to assess your child's health, development, or psychological wellbeing. Do not use Sturdy as a substitute for consultation with qualified healthcare providers.

---

## When to Call Emergency Services

Contact your local emergency services immediately (such as 911 in the United States) if your child is experiencing difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, a serious injury or head trauma, a seizure, a severe allergic reaction, ingestion of a harmful substance, or any situation where their life may be at risk.

Sturdy cannot help in a medical emergency. Do not use the app to assess whether a situation is an emergency. When in doubt, call for help.

---

## When to Contact a Mental Health Professional

Seek guidance from a qualified mental health professional, such as a child psychologist, psychiatrist, licensed therapist, or your child's pediatrician, if your child shows persistent behavioral changes that concern you, expresses thoughts of self-harm or harming others, shows signs of severe anxiety, depression, or withdrawal, has experienced trauma or abuse, displays developmental delays or regressions, or has an eating disorder or disordered eating patterns.

If you as a parent are experiencing a mental health crisis, including thoughts of self-harm, please contact a crisis service immediately. In the United States, you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, text HOME to 741741 to reach the Crisis Text Line, or call 1-800-273-8255.

---

## When to Consult Your Pediatrician

Many parenting challenges that Sturdy helps with — tantrums, bedtime resistance, sibling conflict — are a normal part of child development. However, you should consult your pediatrician if a behavior is significantly more intense, frequent, or prolonged than what seems typical for your child's age, if your child's behavior is affecting their ability to function at school or in social settings, if you notice physical symptoms alongside behavioral changes, or if you have any concerns about your child's development or wellbeing.

Your pediatrician can help determine whether a referral to a specialist is appropriate.

---

## Sturdy's Safety Filter

Sturdy includes an automated safety filter that scans each message for language that may indicate a crisis, such as references to abuse, self-harm, or danger to a child. When the filter detects a potential crisis, it bypasses the normal script generation and instead displays relevant emergency resources and hotline numbers.

This safety filter is not perfect. It may miss crisis signals that are expressed indirectly. It may also occasionally flag messages that are not actually describing a crisis. The filter is a precaution, not a guarantee. If you are in a crisis, do not rely on the app to detect it — reach out to a professional or emergency service directly.

Crisis resources shown by Sturdy are always free, regardless of your subscription status.

---

## Neurodevelopmental Conditions

If your child has been diagnosed with a neurodevelopmental condition such as ADHD, autism spectrum disorder, sensory processing differences, or a learning disability, the communication approaches suggested by Sturdy may or may not be appropriate for your child's specific needs.

We encourage you to share relevant context about your child when requesting a script, so the AI can adjust its suggestions. However, the AI is not a specialist, and its suggestions should be discussed with your child's care team, particularly if your child has an individualized support plan.

---

## Your Judgment Comes First

Sturdy exists to support you, not to replace your judgment. You know your child, your family, and your situation better than any app. If something Sturdy suggests does not feel right, trust yourself.

If you are ever unsure whether your child needs professional attention, err on the side of seeking help. No app can replace the assessment of a qualified professional who can see, hear, and interact with your child directly.

# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: June 15, 2026**
**Last Updated: May 21, 2026**

Sturdy ("we," "us," or "our") is a parenting support application that provides AI-generated communication scripts and guidance. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Sturdy mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using Sturdy, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

---

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create a Sturdy account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. If you sign in through a third-party provider (such as Google or Apple), we receive only the email address and unique identifier provided by that service. We do not receive or store your third-party account password.

### Child Profile Information

To personalize parenting scripts, you may provide information about your child, including their first name, age, and behavioral context such as known triggers or sensitivities. You choose what to share, and you can edit or delete this information at any time.

We do not collect information directly from children. All child-related data is provided by the parent or guardian using the app.

### Parenting Situations You Describe

When you request a script or ask a question, you describe a parenting situation in your own words. This text is sent to our AI system to generate a response. We store a brief summary of the situation type and metadata (such as the mode used and trigger category detected) for the purpose of improving your experience and tracking your usage quota. We do not store the full text of your input after the AI response is generated, except in the case of Question mode responses, which are stored so you can revisit them.

### Usage Data

We collect basic usage information, including which features you use, how many scripts you generate per month, and when you use the Service. This data is used for quota enforcement, product improvement, and troubleshooting.

### Safety Events

If our safety filter detects that a message describes a crisis situation (such as risk of harm to a child or self-harm), we log a brief, anonymized record of the event type for safety monitoring purposes. This record includes the risk level and the type of crisis resource shown but does not include the full text of your message. Safety event records are deleted when you delete your account.

### Crash and Error Data

We use Sentry to collect crash reports and error logs from the app. These reports may include device type, operating system version, and technical details about the error. They do not include the content of your parenting situations or personal information.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Generate personalized parenting scripts and answers to your questions
- Personalize responses based on your child's profile
- Enforce your monthly usage quota on the free plan
- Detect and respond to crisis situations by showing appropriate safety resources
- Identify and fix bugs and errors in the Service
- Improve the quality of AI-generated responses over time
- Process subscription payments through our billing provider

We do not use your conversations to train AI models. Your parenting situations are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to generate real-time responses but are not retained by Anthropic for model training. Sturdy has opted out of any data-sharing programs offered by our AI provider.

---

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share information only with the following service providers, who process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy commitments:

**Supabase** provides our database, authentication, and server infrastructure. Your account data, child profiles, and usage records are stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure.

**Anthropic** provides the AI model (Claude) that generates parenting scripts and answers. When you submit a parenting situation, the text of your message and relevant child profile context (name and age) are sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic processes this data to generate a response and does not retain it for training purposes.

**RevenueCat** manages our subscription billing. If you subscribe to Sturdy+, RevenueCat processes your payment through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. We do not directly handle or store your payment card information.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of a child, or to enforce our Terms of Service.

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data, child profiles, and usage history for as long as your account is active. Saved scripts and Question mode responses are retained until you delete them individually or delete your account.

When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems through cascading deletion. This includes your child profiles, saved scripts, usage history, interaction logs, and safety event records. No anonymized traces remain. Account deletion is irreversible.

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the app. Deletion takes effect immediately. If you have an active Sturdy+ subscription, you must cancel it through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store before deleting your account to avoid continued charges.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (TLS) for all data in transit, row-level security policies in our database to ensure users can only access their own data, and secure authentication through Supabase Auth. While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

---

## Children's Privacy

Sturdy is designed for use by parents and guardians, not by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from anyone under the age of 13. The child profile information stored in Sturdy is provided by the parent or guardian and is used solely to personalize parenting guidance.

If we become aware that a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete the account and associated data promptly.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct inaccurate information, request deletion of your data, object to certain processing of your data, and export your data in a portable format.

You can exercise most of these rights directly within the app. To export your data, use the Data Export feature in Settings. To delete your account and all associated data, use the Delete Account option in Settings.

For any privacy-related requests or questions, contact us at privacy@sturdy.app.

# AI Limitations

**Last Updated: May 21, 2026**

Sturdy uses artificial intelligence to generate parenting scripts, communication suggestions, and answers to parenting questions. We believe in being transparent about what our AI can and cannot do.

---

## How Sturdy's AI Works

When you describe a parenting situation, your message is sent to an AI language model (Anthropic's Claude) along with your child's name and age, if provided. The model generates a response based on its training in child development principles, attachment theory, and practical parenting communication. The response is returned to you in real time, typically within a few seconds.

Sturdy does not store your full message text after the response is generated, with the exception of Question mode responses, which are saved so you can revisit them. The AI does not learn from your individual conversations or retain memory between sessions.

---

## What the AI Does Well

Sturdy's AI is designed to provide calm, practical language for common parenting challenges. It works best when helping you find words for emotionally charged moments such as tantrums, meltdowns, and power struggles. It generates age-appropriate language based on the child's developmental stage. It offers multiple script options so you can choose what feels natural. It provides context about why certain approaches work, grounded in established parenting research.

---

## What the AI Cannot Do

Sturdy's AI has real limitations that you should understand.

It cannot fully understand your specific situation. The AI works from a brief text description of what's happening. It does not see your child, hear their tone, or know your family history beyond what you share in the moment. Important context may be missing.

It cannot diagnose or treat conditions. The AI cannot identify medical, psychological, or developmental conditions. If you notice persistent patterns that concern you, such as developmental delays, behavioral changes, or emotional difficulties, consult a qualified professional.

It may sometimes get it wrong. The AI may misread the situation you describe, suggest an approach that doesn't fit your child's temperament, or produce a script that feels unnatural to you. If a suggestion doesn't feel right, trust your own judgment. You know your child better than any AI system.

It does not replace professional support. For families dealing with trauma, abuse, mental health challenges, neurodevelopmental conditions, or family crises, professional guidance from a therapist, pediatrician, or counselor is essential. Sturdy is a complement to professional support, not a substitute for it.

It is not a crisis service. While Sturdy includes a safety filter that detects potential crisis situations and shows emergency resources, the AI is not trained for crisis intervention. If your child is in immediate danger or you are experiencing a mental health emergency, contact emergency services or a crisis hotline directly.

---

## Neurotype Sensitivity

Sturdy's scripts are designed to work across neurotypes without requiring parents to label or diagnose their child. If you share that your child has a specific neurotype or diagnosis, the AI will incorporate that context. If you don't, the scripts aim to be inclusive and effective regardless.

However, children with specific neurodevelopmental conditions may respond differently to certain communication approaches. If your child has a known condition, we encourage you to share that context in your message and to discuss any Sturdy suggestions with your child's care team.

---

## Continuous Improvement

We regularly review and improve the quality of Sturdy's AI responses. If a script doesn't work for you, your feedback helps us identify patterns and make the system better for all parents.

The AI model itself may be updated over time, which means the style and content of responses may evolve. We aim to make improvements gradually and to maintain the warm, practical tone that defines Sturdy.

---

## Your Role

You are the expert on your child. Sturdy is a tool that offers language and ideas in difficult moments, but every decision about how to respond to your child is yours. Use what resonates. Adapt what almost works. Discard what doesn't fit. The best parenting script is the one that feels true to you and your relationship with your child.
